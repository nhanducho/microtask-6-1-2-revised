Key takeaway

One signal can have several plausible meanings.

The delay was visible, but its cause was not. New information ruled out some explanations without revealing one certain cause.

Ambiguity versus uncertainty

Ambiguity

You are unsure what the available information means, which cause applies, or how the situation should be interpreted.

Uncertainty

The possible outcomes are understood, but you do not know which one will occur.

A practical decision pattern

  1. 1
    Observe the signal

    Notice a delayed shipment or an unusual tracking status.

  2. 2
    Consider several explanations

    Do not confuse the first plausible explanation with the only one.

  3. 3
    Seek evidence and act robustly

    Reduce ambiguity where possible, then choose an action that can tolerate being wrong.

Supply-chain connection: A delayed supplier shipment may point to production, transport, customs, capacity, or inaccurate data. The same signal need not have the same cause.
